隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 27 日
适用 App：VoiceCamera / 喊拍（iOS，Bundle ID：com.ace.voicecamera）

本政策说明 VoiceCamera 当前版本如何使用权限和设备上的信息。若后续版本的数据处理方式发生实质变化，我们会在发布前更新本政策。

1. 我们处理的信息

相机、麦克风与您创建的媒体

VoiceCamera 使用相机提供预览、拍照和录像，并使用麦克风为录像和实况照片录制声音。照片、视频和实况照片由您在 App 内主动拍摄；应用不会将这些内容上传到开发者运营的服务器。

在创建或保存视频、实况照片时，应用可能会在设备的临时存储中短暂创建媒体文件，以完成编码和导出。保存完成后，照片和视频由您的照片图库管理。

照片图库

应用仅在您保存媒体时请求“仅添加”照片图库权限，用于把您创建的照片、视频或实况照片存入图库。此权限不允许应用浏览、读取或分析您现有的照片图库内容。您可以在 iOS 设置中更改此权限；拒绝授权时，应用无法将新媒体保存到照片图库。

语音识别

当您启用语音命令时，应用会将麦克风音频提供给 Apple 的语音识别框架，以识别“开始录像”“停止录像”“拍照”等命令。识别结果用于当前 App 会话中的界面反馈和命令执行，不由应用持久保存。语音识别的可用性和处理方式可能取决于您的设备、所选语言以及 Apple 系统服务；请参阅 Apple 的适用隐私说明以了解其服务处理方式。

本地设置与使用次数

应用在您的设备上保存相机设置、已完成引导状态、语音命令偏好和免费使用次数等本地偏好。这些信息不用于创建开发者账户，也不会由应用同步到开发者服务器。

一次性购买

VoiceCamera Pro 是由 Apple 处理的一次性、非消耗型 App 内购买。应用会使用 StoreKit 交易权益来判断 Pro 功能是否已解锁。支付信息由 Apple 处理，应用不会收集您的支付卡号或 Apple Account 凭据。

2. 不追踪、不出售、不投放广告

当前版本不包含广告 SDK、分析 SDK、开发者运营的网络服务或跨 App/网站跟踪技术。我们不会出售您的个人信息，也不会为定向广告跟踪您。

3. 保存期限与您的控制

您保存到照片图库的媒体由您通过照片 App 和 iOS 设置控制。临时媒体文件仅用于完成拍摄、编码或导出流程。本地设置会保留在您的设备上，直到您删除 App 或清除其数据（适用时）。

4. 安全与儿童

我们采取合理措施使应用以本地处理为主，但没有任何电子传输或存储方式能够保证绝对安全。请根据 App Store 年龄分级及适用法律使用本应用；如您认为儿童向我们提供了信息，请通过下方邮箱联系我们。

5. 政策更新与联系我们

我们可能会为反映功能、法律或实践变化而更新本政策。更新后会在本页调整生效日期。有关隐私、支持或本政策的问题，请发送邮件至 cumtx1024@163.com。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026
App: VoiceCamera / 喊拍 (iOS, bundle ID com.ace.voicecamera)

This policy describes how the current version of VoiceCamera uses permissions and information on your device. We will update it before a future release materially changes these practices.

1. Information used by the app

Camera, microphone, and media you create

VoiceCamera uses the Camera for preview, photos, and video, and uses the Microphone to record sound for video and Live Photos. Photos, videos, and Live Photos are created when you initiate capture in the app. The app does not upload that content to a developer-operated server.

While a video or Live Photo is created or saved, the app may create temporary media files in device storage for encoding and export. After saving, your media is managed by your Photos library.

Photos library

The app requests add-only Photos permission only when you save media, so it can add the photo, video, or Live Photo you created to your library. Add-only permission does not allow the app to browse, read, or analyze your existing Photos library. If permission is denied, the app cannot save new media to Photos.

Speech recognition

When voice commands are enabled, the app provides microphone audio to Apple’s speech-recognition framework to recognize commands such as “Start Recording,” “Stop Recording,” and “Take Photo.” Recognition results are used for in-session feedback and command execution and are not persisted by the app. Availability and processing may depend on your device, selected language, and Apple system services; consult Apple’s applicable privacy information for its service processing.

Local preferences and usage count

The app stores camera settings, onboarding completion, voice-command preferences, and free-use counts locally on your device. This information does not create a developer account and is not synced by the app to a developer server.

One-time purchase

VoiceCamera Pro is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase processed by Apple. The app uses StoreKit entitlements to determine whether Pro features are unlocked. Apple handles payment information; the app does not collect payment-card numbers or Apple Account credentials.

2. No tracking, sale, or advertising

The current version contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, developer-operated network service, or cross-app/website tracking technology. We do not sell personal information or track you for targeted advertising.

3. Retention and your control

Media you save to Photos remains under your control through Photos and iOS settings. Temporary media files are used only for capture, encoding, or export. Local settings remain on your device until you delete the app or clear its data where applicable.

4. Security and children

We use reasonable measures to keep processing local, but no electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Use the app in accordance with its App Store age rating and applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us at the address below.

5. Changes and contact

We may update this policy to reflect changes in features, law, or practice. The effective date above will be revised when we do. For privacy, support, or policy questions, email cumtx1024@163.com.
