开始使用

  1. 打开 App 后，按引导授予所需权限。
  2. 您可以使用屏幕上的快门与录制按钮，也可以使用语音命令。
  3. 尝试说出“开始录像”“停止录像”或“拍照”。不同语言的可用命令以 App 内设置和提示为准。

权限说明

相机和麦克风

相机用于预览、拍照和录像；麦克风用于录像音频与语音命令。若未授权，请前往 设置 → VoiceCamera 开启权限后返回 App。

语音识别

语音识别不可用、被拒绝或当前语言/设备不支持时，您仍可使用屏幕上的手动控件拍照和录像。请在 iOS 设置中检查“语音识别”权限后重试。

照片

应用只会在保存时请求“仅添加照片”权限。拒绝该权限不会影响预览或拍摄，但无法把新创建的媒体存入照片图库。

照片、视频与实况照片

完成拍摄后，应用会将您主动保存的媒体写入照片图库。若保存失败，请确认照片权限、可用存储空间，并稍后再试。实况照片需要在快门后继续收集短暂画面，请在保存完成前保持 App 位于前台。

购买与恢复

VoiceCamera Pro 是一次性购买，并非订阅。若您已购买但 Pro 未解锁，请在 App 的付费页面或设置页面选择“恢复购买”，并确保设备使用的是原购买所用的 Apple Account。付款与退款由 Apple 处理。

Getting started

  1. Open the app and grant the requested permissions during onboarding.
  2. Use the on-screen shutter and record controls, or use voice commands.
  3. Try “Start Recording,” “Stop Recording,” or “Take Photo.” Available commands vary by the language and settings shown in the app.

Permissions

Camera and Microphone

The Camera is used for preview, photos, and video; the Microphone is used for video sound and voice commands. If access is denied, open Settings → VoiceCamera, enable the permission, then return to the app.

Speech Recognition

If speech recognition is unavailable, denied, or unsupported for the current device/language, you can still use the manual on-screen camera controls. Check the Speech Recognition permission in iOS Settings, then try again.

Photos

The app requests add-only Photos permission only when saving. Denying it does not stop preview or capture, but it prevents new media from being added to your Photos library.

Photos, videos, and Live Photos

After capture, the app writes media that you choose to save to your Photos library. If saving fails, check Photos permission and available storage, then try again. A Live Photo continues collecting a short moment after the shutter; keep the app in the foreground until saving completes.

Purchases and restore

VoiceCamera Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. If you have purchased it but Pro is not unlocked, choose Restore Purchase from the paywall or Settings and make sure the device uses the Apple Account that made the original purchase. Apple handles payment and refunds.
